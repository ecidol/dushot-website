Two views. Your composition.

Keep your
iPhone upright.

Dushot puts portrait and landscape framing in view together, with separate controls for the composition you’re working on.

  • See both compositions.

    The portrait preview fills the screen. A floating landscape preview lets you keep the other orientation in view.

  • Place the landscape preview.

    Move and resize the floating landscape view to arrange your framing workspace.

  • Adjust each view.

    Select a preview to adjust its framing, focus, and exposure. Work on the portrait and landscape compositions separately.
