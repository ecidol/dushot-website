Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Dushot for iPhone

Dushot processes camera content on your iPhone. The app does not send your photos, videos, audio, usage information or device information to the developer. There are no accounts, advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs or tracking in the app.

Who is responsible

Dushot is provided by Ge Lin. For privacy questions or requests, contact gymbtm@outlook.com.

Camera content and permissions

  • Camera: used to display previews and capture photos and video on your device.
  • Microphone: used to record audio for video when you allow access.
  • Photos — add access: used to save completed captures to your system Photos library.
  • Photos — read access: requested separately when you choose to check previously saved items during recovery from an interrupted save. Dushot uses the saved asset identifiers to check those items; it does not upload your photo library.

You can change these permissions in iOS Settings. If you deny a permission, the feature that needs it may be unavailable. Dushot does not request location, contacts or advertising tracking permission.

Information stored on your iPhone

The app stores your preferences, floating preview layout and onboarding state locally. It also keeps local technical information needed for capability checks, capture timing, error diagnosis and recovery. This can include camera capabilities, iOS version, capture timestamps, file identifiers and save status. These records are not transmitted to us.

Captures awaiting a successful save are kept in the app’s local recovery storage, which may temporarily contain the only recoverable copy. After Photos confirms a successful save, Dushot cleans up its extra media copy. If a save fails or its result is uncertain, the app retains the recoverable material and minimal recovery records until the issue is resolved or you choose to discard pending material.

Your choices and deletion

You can retry pending saves or use the app’s discard control to remove pending material. Discarding an unsaved capture can permanently remove its only copy. Delete captures saved to Photos using the Photos app; iOS controls its Recently Deleted behavior. Deleting Dushot removes its local app data, including unsaved captures, preferences and local diagnostic records. It does not delete captures already saved to Photos.

Dushot does not operate a cloud media service. If you enable iCloud Photos or device backups, Apple may sync or back up data according to your Apple settings and Apple’s privacy policy. This is separate from Dushot uploading data to the developer.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive the email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request and resolve the issue, not for advertising or profiling. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Support messages are retained only as needed to handle the request and any applicable recordkeeping obligations; you may request deletion by emailing us.

Email is handled through our email provider, Microsoft Outlook, which processes messages to deliver and store correspondence. We do not sell your personal information or share your camera content with advertisers.

This website and other services

This privacy page and the Dushot website are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting them sends ordinary web-request information, such as your IP address and browser information, to GitHub. We do not add analytics, advertising scripts or tracking cookies to this page. GitHub handles hosting information under its privacy statement. App Store purchases and any diagnostics you choose to share with Apple are handled under Apple’s policies.

Privacy rights and children

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to its processing, or contact your local privacy regulator. For information you have sent to support, contact us using the email above. We cannot retrieve camera content or other app data that remains solely on your iPhone.

Dushot does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app. If a child has sent personal information to support, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its deletion.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date if Dushot’s privacy practices change. Any new access that requires permission will be requested through the appropriate iOS permission flow.
